## Ships

An archive behaves like a Finder folder, reliably, for anyone: hardened against hostile archives, documented, and proven by a public beta. No new features.

## Done when

- [ ] The readers survive 24 hours of fuzzing and the hostile-archive suite
- [ ] A two-week public beta ends with zero open crash or data-loss reports
- [ ] Help and the landing page let a stranger start without asking anyone

- Any feature not already shipped in v0.4
- Writing RAR or 7z
- Localization beyond English
