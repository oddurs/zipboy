## Ships

Zipboy works from the Finder as well as in its own window: Quick Look shows what's in an archive, Quick Actions compress and extract, updates arrive by themselves, and every flow works with VoiceOver and the keyboard alone.

## Done when

- [ ] Space on an archive in Finder shows its contents
- [ ] Compress with Zipboy and Extract Here appear in Finder's Quick Actions
- [ ] An installed copy updates itself to a newer release
- [ ] Every flow in the README can be done with VoiceOver and the keyboard only

## Explicitly not in this milestone

- Mac App Store distribution
- A command-line tool
