Zipboy Help

Browse, edit, verify, and extract archives like a Finder folder.

Opening & Browsing Archives

Open any ZIP, RAR, 7z, TAR, Gzip, Bzip2, XZ, Zstandard, LHA, CAB, ISO, CPIO, or XAR archive with ⌘O or by dragging it onto Zipboy. Press Space for Quick Look or double-click a nested archive to open it inside Zipboy.

Adding, Renaming, & Deleting Files

Drag files or folders into the list, or press ⌘⇧A to add files and ⌘⇧N for a new folder. Hovering a dragged item over a collapsed folder expands it automatically. Press Return to rename and Delete to remove items. Every change supports ⌘Z Undo and ⌘S Save.

Extracting & Dragging Out

Drag any selection straight to Finder, press ⌘E to extract to your configured default folder, or press ⌥⌘E to choose a destination folder.

Passwords & AES-256 Encryption

Choose File > Set Password… to protect an archive with AES-256 encryption when saved, or File > Remove Password to save it unencrypted. Check Remember password in Keychain to reopen protected archives without retyping the password.

Integrity Verification & Checksums

Choose File > Verify Archive (⌘⇧T) to test every entry in the archive for corruption without extracting files to disk. The right-hand Inspector (⌥⌘I) displays CRC-32 and SHA-256 checksums for selected files.

Exporting & Filename Encodings

Choose File > Export… (⌘⇧E) to export an archive as .zip, .tar.gz, .tar.xz, .tar, or .7z. If an older archive has garbled non-UTF-8 filenames, choose an encoding under View > Text Encoding.
